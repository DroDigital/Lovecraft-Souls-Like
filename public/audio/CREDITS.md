# Sound credits

The game's recorded sounds (playtest round 6) are all from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), every one
dedicated to the public domain under [Creative Commons 0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(CC0 1.0 Universal): free to use, change and share, for any purpose, with no attribution required. They
are credited here anyway. Most come from Craig Smith's library of vintage film and radio effects and a few
field recordists: rarely downloaded recordings, not the stock sounds heard in many games. Each file is a
cut of one recording treated for the game (below), encoded as mono MP3 (ambience beds at 32 kHz, 64 kbps,
looped by crossfading their end into their start; the rest at 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps). `amb/` holds the beds,
`sfx/` everything else. The engine plays them with more variation still (render/audio/sampler.ts).

The title's theme (`../music/subterranean-pulse.mp3`) came with the project.
